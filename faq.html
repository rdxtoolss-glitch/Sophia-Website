Frequently Asked Questions

Find technical and product answers regarding Sophia's creator, dual personas, Pakistani Urdu default fluency, WhatsApp integration, Developer VS Code assistant, and Google OAuth compliance.

Origin & Creator

Who created and built Sophia AI?

Sophia AI was created, built, and founded by Muhammad Junaid (@rdxtoolss-glitch) at Mobo Devs Studio (Mobo Devs) (hosted at mobodevs.net). It is distributed as free and open-source software under the permissive MIT License.

How is Sophia different from web chatbots or Windows Copilot?

Web chatbots are sandboxed inside browser tabs—they cannot make WhatsApp calls, edit your local VS Code workspace, or type essays into Microsoft Word. Windows Copilot is largely a web sidebar. Sophia is an autonomous desktop system featuring sub-400ms voice turnaround, continuous screen perception, and 9 specialized Windows capabilities running hands-free desktop tasks.

Spoken Language & Dual Personas

What is Sophia's default language and how does language switching work?

Sophia's foundational default operational language is Pakistani Urdu (اردو). She speaks with natural cadence, proper cultural warmth, and feminine grammatical inflections (بولتی ہوں, کرتی ہوں, سنتی ہوں).

Whenever requested (e.g. "Next you have to talk to me in English" or "Habla en español"), Sophia immediately switches languages and remembers your preference permanently across sessions. She has native fluency across all world languages and accents.

What are the dual personas (Sophia & Leo)?

Sophia includes two distinct vocal personas selectable in Settings:

  • Sophia (Female Persona): Warm, collaborative, and empathetic, with natural feminine Urdu verbal inflection.
  • Leo (Male Persona): Direct, crisp, and technical, with natural masculine Urdu verbal inflection (بولتا ہوں, کرتا ہوں).

WhatsApp Desktop & Developer Tooling

Can Sophia make voice and video calls on WhatsApp?

Yes. Sophia directly interfaces with the official Windows WhatsApp desktop client. You can say "Call Hamza on WhatsApp" or "واٹس ایپ پر حمزہ کو وائس کال ملاؤ", and Sophia executes the call hands-free. She can also answer incoming calls, mute/hold audio, search chat histories, draft messages, and send confirmations.

How does the Developer Assistant integrate with Visual Studio Code?

Sophia writes and edits code files directly on your disk, inspects project directories, and runs terminal tests. When finished, she focuses your active VS Code editor right at the modified file and line number, reciting a concise summary over voice rather than reading long walls of code.

How fast is Sophia's typing into Microsoft Word and Notepad?

Sophia houses an ultra-fast document authoring engine capable of typing into Microsoft Word and Notepad at sub-15ms speed. She can write everything from short introductions to comprehensive multi-page academic essays and 100+ page comprehensive theses (hundreds of thousands of words) in seconds.

Privacy, DPAPI Vault & Google OAuth Compliance

Where are my memories and conversation histories stored?

All conversational memories, user preferences, and custom spoken language states reside in a local SQLite database at %APPDATA%\Sophia\data\sophia.db, encrypted with the Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI). Encryption keys are tied directly to your Windows user logon account; no data is ever uploaded to centralized cloud analytics servers.

Why does Sophia request Google OAuth 2.0 access and how is data handled?

Google OAuth 2.0 is an optional integration used to authenticate the desktop user and power Google Workspace features (Google Calendar scheduling, Google Tasks, and Gmail draft composition).

All OAuth refresh tokens and retrieved data reside locally on your PC (in %APPDATA%\Sophia\data\google_tokens.json). We never sell, lease, or share Google user data with advertisers, third-party brokers, or external AI model trainers. Sophia adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
