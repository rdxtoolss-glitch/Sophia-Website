Getting Started

First-Time Setup on Windows

Getting Sophia running on your Windows PC takes less than two minutes.

STEP 01

Download & Install

Download Sophia-Setup-2.2.0-x64.exe from our official download page. Run the installer and follow the prompt. Sophia will automatically configure system tray integration.

Go to Downloads →
STEP 02

Audio Permissions

On first launch, Windows will request permission to access your microphone. Click Allow. Sophia works best with high-quality laptop array mics or external USB microphones.

Windows Settings > Privacy > Microphone: ON
STEP 03

Summon Your Assistant

Press Ctrl + Space anywhere in Windows to bring up the holographic 3D particle HUD, or simply start speaking in Pakistani Urdu or English.

Hotkey: [Ctrl] + [Space]

Productivity

Keyboard Shortcuts & Hotkeys

Control Sophia quickly from anywhere in Windows with global hotkeys.

Summon / Dismiss HUD

GLOBAL
Ctrl + Space

Opens or minimizes the holographic 3D particle HUD window over your active applications.

Toggle Microphone Mute

PRIVACY
Ctrl + M

Instantly mutes or unmutes your microphone input. When muted, the 3D particle orb turns amber.

Instant Interruption

AUDIO CONTROL
Esc

Instantly cuts off Sophia's active speech response and clears the audio buffer queue immediately.

Customization

Sophia vs. Leo Personas

Switch between two distinct voice personas depending on your work style.

Sophia (Default Persona)

FEMININE

Warm, encouraging, and highly collaborative. Sophia speaks fluent Pakistani Urdu with authentic feminine grammatical agreement (کرتی ہوں، بولتی ہوں), as well as fluent English. Perfect for daily planning, creative brainstorming, and hands-free productivity.

Voice Command: "صوفیہ، مجھے آج کے ٹاسک یاد کرواؤ"

Leo (Executive Persona)

MASCULINE

Concise, technical, and analytical. Leo responds with crisp technical clarity in both Urdu and English, focusing heavily on developer workflows, system diagnostics, code audits, and executive summaries.

Voice Command: "Leo, switch to developer mode and inspect auth.py"
Support

Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions

Sophia is not hearing my voice or audio level is low

1. Open Windows Settings > Sound and verify your default input device has its volume slider set to at least 80%.

2. In the Sophia HUD settings tray, make sure the microphone input meter bounces green when you speak.

3. Ensure no other exclusive-mode audio application is locking your microphone device.

How does WhatsApp Desktop VoIP calling work?

Sophia interacts directly with the official WhatsApp for Windows desktop client from the Microsoft Store. Ensure you are signed into WhatsApp on your PC. You can then say "Call [Contact Name] on WhatsApp" or "[Contact Name] کو واٹس ایپ پر کال کرو" to connect hands-free.

How do I connect Google Calendar, Tasks, and Gmail?

Click on Connect Google Account in the Sophia settings window. A standard secure Google OAuth 2.0 browser window will open. Once authorized, tokens are stored locally on your PC encrypted by Windows DPAPI. Sophia only accesses the specific calendar and task data you authorize.

Where are my personal memories and preferences stored?

All memory files are kept strictly on your Windows device inside %APPDATA%\Sophia\data\sophia.db. They are encrypted using the Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI) and tied to your logon credentials. No personal memories or files are transmitted to cloud telemetry servers.
