First Run Walkthrough

Getting Started in 4 Easy Steps

You will be conversing with Sophia in less than two minutes.

1. Run Setup Wizard

Execute Sophia-Setup-2.2.0-x64.exe and follow the NSIS prompts. The app installs to $LOCALAPPDATA\Programs\Sophia.

2. Account & Workspace Sign-In

Launch the app from your Start Menu. Optionally click Sign in with Google to connect Google Calendar, Tasks, and Gmail drafts.

3. Calibrate Microphone & Audio

Sophia verifies your audio input device and primes studio-grade echo cancellation to prevent speaker loopback and echo.

4. Choose Persona & Speak

Select Sophia (Urdu/multilingual) or Leo. Press Ctrl + Space and talk naturally!

Windows SmartScreen & Defender Notice

Because Sophia AI is an independent open-source release, Microsoft Windows Defender SmartScreen may display a prompt: "Windows protected your PC — Microsoft Defender SmartScreen prevented an unrecognized app from starting."

This is standard Windows behavior for newly compiled community binaries. Click "More info" and then select "Run anyway".
