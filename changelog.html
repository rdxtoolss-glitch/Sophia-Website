Product Evolution

Release Notes & Changelog

Track every major feature rollout, speed improvement, and usability enhancement added to Sophia AI by Muhammad Junaid at Mobo Devs Studio.

v2.2.0

CURRENT PRODUCTION
October 2026

Major production release introducing native WhatsApp VoIP calling, VS Code integration, ultra-fast Word document authoring, a 3D audio-reactive HUD, and authentic Pakistani Urdu fluency.

  • +
    WhatsApp Desktop Calling & Messaging: Make hands-free voice and video calls, answer calls, mute/hold, and send messages through the official WhatsApp Windows client.
  • +
    Visual Studio Code Integration: Edit project files, navigate codebases, run tests, and automatically focus target files and lines directly in VS Code.
  • +
    Ultra-Fast Word & Document Typing: Rapid document typing engine writes comprehensive essays, theses, and business reports straight into Microsoft Word or Notepad at sub-15ms speed.
  • +
    Audio-Reactive 3D Particle HUD: Living 3D visual orb responds dynamically to speech amplitudes, with live visual indicators for background actions.
  • +
    Pakistani Urdu (اردو) Fluency: Authentic spoken Urdu with natural feminine grammar (بولتی ہوں، کرتی ہوں), paired with dual personas (Sophia vs Leo) and English support.
  • +
    Hardware-Tied Windows DPAPI Security: Local encryption tied to your Windows logon protects preferences, notes, and token caches directly on your SSD.

v2.1.0

AUDIO PERFORMANCE & STABILITY
September 2026

Comprehensive audio engine improvements delivering zero talk-over interruptions and studio-grade echo cancellation.

  • +
    Echo Cancellation Warmup: Eliminates laptop speaker feedback loops so Sophia never interrupts herself during speech playout.
  • +
    Instant Barge-In Queue Flushing: Audio immediately pauses and discards buffered speech as soon as the user starts speaking.
  • +
    Dynamic Voice Endpointing: Enhanced speech activity detection identifies speech ends without awkward pauses.
  • ✓
    Stability Improvement: Resolved concurrency locks on local databases during rapid multi-task operations.

v2.0.0

REAL-TIME FOUNDATION
August 2026
  • +
    Full migration to real-time bidirectional audio streaming for sub-400ms conversational response time.
  • +
    Dual persona foundation introduced: Sophia (collaborative feminine) and Leo (analytical masculine).
  • +
    Google Workspace integration: hands-free scheduling for Google Calendar, Google Tasks, and Gmail drafts.
